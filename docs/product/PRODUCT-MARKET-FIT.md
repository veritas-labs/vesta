# Vesta — Product-Market Fit

## The Problem

Running a household is a coordination problem masquerading as a set of isolated tasks.

Every household — regardless of shape — has the same fundamental challenge: **multiple people, overlapping
responsibilities, shared resources, zero formal training, and no project manager.**

The current solutions are inadequate:

| What people use today                                                      | Why it fails                                                                                |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Shared mental models** ("I just know what needs doing")                  | Breaks down the moment life gets complex or someone assumes someone else handled it         |
| **Group chats** (WhatsApp, iMessage)                                       | Tasks get buried in conversation. No accountability. No structure.                          |
| **Sticky notes / whiteboards**                                             | Single point of failure. No integration with anything. Faded by Tuesday.                    |
| **Single-purpose apps** (chore trackers, expense splitters, reminder apps) | Fragmented. Each solves one piece. Nobody wants 6 apps to run a house.                      |
| **All-in-one household platforms** (Cozi, OurHome, etc.)                   | Attempt to do everything. End up doing nothing well. Assume a nuclear family. Cluttered UX. |

## The Gap

There is no product that says: **"Tell us what kind of household you are, and we'll give you exactly the tools that make
sense."**

Every existing product starts from a template and forces you into it. Vesta starts from the user's reality and builds
outward.

**The gap in one sentence:** Modular, adaptive household management that respects that every home is different — without
requiring a weekend of setup or a computer science degree.

## Why Now?

Three converging trends make this the right moment:

### 1. Household composition is diversifying

Multi-generational living is rising. Flat-sharing extends into the 30s and 40s. Single-person households are the
fastest-growing demographic in many countries. The "traditional family" template that most apps assume is no longer the
majority case.

### 2. Attention economy fatigue

People are actively seeking tools that do one thing well rather than platforms that demand constant engagement. The
shift from "engagement" to "utility" in software is real. Vesta can ride this wave.

### 3. The "last mile" of home management is underserved

Big tech has conquered music, video, shopping, and communication. Household coordination is one of the last areas where
people still rely on ad-hoc, manual processes. It's an unglamorous problem — which is exactly why it's still unsolved.

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor            | What they do well               | Where they fall short                                           |
|-----------------------|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Cozi**              | Shared family calendar, lists   | Dated UX. Assumes nuclear family. No modularity.                |
| **OurHome**           | Gamified chores for families    | Child-focused. Doesn't serve non-family households.             |
| **Tody**              | Cleaning schedule visualisation | Single-purpose. No multi-user coordination.                     |
| **Sweepy**            | Chores with rotation            | Narrow scope. Limited household types.                          |
| **Todoist / AnyTask** | General task management         | Not designed for households. No shared accountability built in. |

**Vesta's differentiation:** Modularity + household-shape awareness. No competitor starts from "what kind of household
is this?" and adapts.

## Definition of Product-Market Fit (for Vesta)

We'll know we have PMF when:

1. **Activation:** >40% of sign-ups complete onboarding AND add their first module within 24 hours.
2. **Retention:** >30% of users are still active in their household after 30 days.
3. **Qualitative signal:** Users describe Vesta to others without us prompting them. Specifically, they mention it by
   describing what it *does for them*, not what it *is*.

---

## Current Position & Honest Acknowledgment

- **Market size:** Not yet validated. We have no data on how many households in our target market currently use a
  digital household-management tool. This does not block v1 but should inform positioning before any market-facing push.
- **Competitive moat:** None at present. Modularity is replicable. We currently lack defensibility — no network effects,
  no ecosystem, no proprietary data. This is acknowledged and expected to *emerge* as we learn what resonates with real
  users. We are not pretending otherwise.
